What the app is

The Biological Reboot turns the book’s framework into a calm, local-first daily practice. Its core reading, Map, Stack, lessons, and book work on your device with no app account, tracking, subscription, or in-app purchase. The optional Ask feature requires a funded Anthropic API key and sends only the question and context disclosed in its consent sheet.

Common questions

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open the menu (top left), choose Apple Health, and grant read access. The app reads sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, deep sleep, cardio fitness, and steps. It is read-only and never writes anything back. Permission is per-data-type and can be changed anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Do I need to connect Apple Health to use the app?

No. You can log sleep and daily inputs by hand and use the book, the Map, and the daily read without a wearable. Apple Health simply fills in more of the picture automatically.

What is the optional assistant, and do I need a key?

The assistant is optional. To use it you paste your own Anthropic API key, which stays on your device. You can ask questions in plain language, or attach a photo of a supplement label or a lab result for the framework’s honest read. It contacts Anthropic only when you choose to ask, using your key. You can get a key at platform.claude.com.

Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

No. The app has no subscription and no in-app purchases. If you use the optional assistant, Anthropic bills your own Anthropic account directly under their pricing. The app never sees your payment details and takes no share.

Is my data private?

The app is local-first: there is no publisher server, account, analytics, or third-party tracking. Ask sends your explicit question or attachment plus the context named in its consent sheet only when you choose to ask; encrypted backup goes only to the destination you choose. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

How do I delete my data?

Because everything is stored locally, deleting the app removes all of your data. You can also reset your information from within the app’s Settings.

Is this medical advice?

No. The Biological Reboot is a wellness and education tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a licensed professional. Always talk to your clinician before making changes to medications or supplements, and seek professional care for any medical concern.
